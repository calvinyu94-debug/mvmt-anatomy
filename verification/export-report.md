## 6. What the model did that the inventory did not predict

- **Mirroring by negative scale, with shared mesh data.** Twin pairs: mirror-instance 938, separate-data 8, shared-data-not-mirror 14, unpaired 24. The `.l` side of a mirror-instance pair is the same datablock as the `.r` side under a reflecting transform. Every object is baked to world space and the winding reversed where the determinant is negative, so every exported node has an identity transform and outward normals on both sides. Decimation runs once per datablock and is baked twice.
- **Modifiers.** articular/SOLIDIFY 300, articular/SUBSURF 238, muscular/SOLIDIFY 51, muscular/SUBSURF 99, skeletal/BOOLEAN 6, skeletal/MIRROR 1, skeletal/SUBSURF 32. The base meshes the inventory counted are cages for most ligaments: the anterior talofibular ligament is two triangles before its Subdivision and Solidify run. Skeletal, muscular and articular objects are therefore exported from their viewport-evaluated mesh (2,938,071 -> 3,378,144 triangles). Insertions are the exception and stay as flat base-mesh patches at full resolution (their modifiers would take 63,843 triangles to 931,484 for a 0.5 mm skin); a viewer should draw the insertion layer with a small depth offset.
- **Inside-out source meshes.** 8 closed objects had negative signed volume after the bake, i.e. inward-facing winding in the atlas itself, on both sides. They were flipped: `Acetabular labrum.l`, `Acetabular labrum.r`, `Costotransverse ligament.l`, `Costotransverse ligament.r`, `Glenoid labrum.l`, `Glenoid labrum.r`, `Humeral head of extensor carpi ulnaris.l`, `Humeral head of extensor carpi ulnaris.r`.
- **One-sided objects.** 26 objects have no contralateral twin (listed in §2), 24 of them insertion footprints painted on one side only.
- **Upstream side tags are wrong as well as incomplete.** Left tarsal bones are filed under `Right foot`. Division tags are used with the side word stripped; the side comes only from the name suffix.
- **The exporter drops a few degenerate triangles.** Manifest counts are read back from each file's index accessors, not from the meshes handed to the exporter, so they are what a viewer will load.
